Frequently Asked Questions

What is Hubaal Water Company, and what does it do?

Hubaal Water Company is a leading provider of clean and safe drinking water in Puntland.

Where is Hubaal Water Company located, and what areas does it serve?

The company is headquartered in Bossaso, a strategic port city in Puntland. From there, it distributes water to urban centers and remote areas across the region.

What makes Hubaal Water’s branding unique?

Hubaal Water’s brand emphasizes reliability, purity, and community impact. It uses clean blue tones to symbolize purity and trust, while its bold logo represents strength and dependability.

What is the vision of Hubaal Water Company?

The company aims to be the most trusted and innovative provider of clean, safe, and affordable water in Puntland, improving the health and well-being of communities.

What key services does Hubaal Water offer?

Hubaal Water provides bulk water delivery, water storage tank installation, emergency water supply for drought-affected areas, and customized water solutions for industries with large-scale water needs.
